# Kion FinOps+ · Design CAB

Presentation for practical assignment 2 (Design CAB), group KB-307.

**Live:** https://nazya-why.github.io/kion-finops-deck/

A single-page HTML deck: a ray-traced black hole background (WebGL2), liquid-glass slides
that scroll vertically, and the service architecture, network and data flow diagrams.

## Controls

- Arrow keys, Space, Page Up/Down, mouse wheel or swipe — next / previous slide
- Click a tick on the right rail — jump to that slide
- Click a diagram — open it full screen; click again to zoom
- `F` — full screen

## Files

- `index.html` — the whole deck (styles, background renderer and slide logic inline)
- `diagrams/` — the three diagrams shown on slides 3, 5 and 10
